Megohm Soak Support

Clara Vaughn

Megohm Soak is a free iOS app for logging a 10-minute insulation resistance test on a motor winding with your own megohmmeter, with the motor isolated. Enter or dial in each reading, set the winding temperature, and the app corrects the 1-minute value to 40 °C, works out the polarization index and compares it with the minimum for the winding and insulation class. Always discharge the winding after the test. Questions about the app: clara.vaughn45@126.com.
