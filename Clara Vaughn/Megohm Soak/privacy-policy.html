Megohm Soak keeps each motor's name, rating and insulation details, and every insulation test you record (test voltage, winding temperature and the readings from 0.5 to 10 minutes) on this iPhone. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing a test uses the system share sheet and only sends the text you choose to share.
