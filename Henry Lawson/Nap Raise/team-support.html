Support for Nap Raise

Nap Raise is a free iOS app made by Henry Lawson. Contact: henry.lawson82@sina.com.

Brush a panel with its lie to raise it. Brushing against the lie, or brushing a panel that is already raised, leaves that panel as it is and says so. Turn a flat panel to change its lie. Steam lays every panel flat again.
