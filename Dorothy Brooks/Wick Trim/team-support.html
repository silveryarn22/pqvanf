Wick Trim Support

Wick Trim lets you drag a cut on a candle wick. A cut 8 to 22 above the wax pool is kept. A cut into the pool does not change the wick. A kept trim stays until the pool is raised.

The app is free. It does not use an account. Wicks stay on this iPhone.
