Privacy Policy

Wick Trim is an offline tool for leaving a candle wick stub above the wax pool. It does not track you.

The wicks you name stay on this iPhone. The app does not require an account. There is nothing to buy. The app does not collect personal data and does not send your wicks anywhere.
