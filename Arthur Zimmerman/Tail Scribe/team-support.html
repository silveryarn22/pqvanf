Tail Scribe Support

Arthur Zimmerman

Tail Scribe keeps each dovetail on this iPhone. The tail is scribed only from a pin that has been cut. Sawing on the line after the tail is scribed makes the joint pull tight. Sawing past the line leaves the tail loose. Sawing before the tail is scribed makes the tail scrap, and the pin stays. A tight or scrap joint does not change again. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing uses the system share sheet and only sends the joint name and whether it is tight, loose, scrap, or not sawed.
