Marbling Gall Privacy Policy

Helen Keller

Marbling Gall keeps each bath name, each bead size in millimetres, and whether that bead is tight, spread, or sealed on this iPhone. Gall opens only a tight bead. A new drop while a bead is still tight seals the latest tight bead. A sheet is saved only when no bead is tight. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing a sheet uses the system share sheet and only sends the text you choose to share.
