Marbling Gall is a free iOS app for a marbling shop log. Drop a tight color bead, touch gall to spread it, and lay a sheet only when no bead is still tight. If another drop lands while a bead is tight, the latest tight bead is sealed and gall will not open it later. The last saved sheet cannot be deleted. Questions about the app: helen.keller67@126.com.
