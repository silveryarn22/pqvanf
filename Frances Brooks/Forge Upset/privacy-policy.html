Forge Upset Privacy Policy

Frances Brooks

Forge Upset keeps each bar name, the heat of the end, the length, whether the length is locked, and each upset struck from a white end on this iPhone. Only a white end shortens the bar. A strike on the middle bends the bar and leaves the length. A strike that is not white only marks the bar. Quench locks the length. The last upset stays. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing an upset uses the system share sheet and only sends the text you choose to share.
