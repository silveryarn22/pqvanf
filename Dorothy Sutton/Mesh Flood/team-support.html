Mesh Flood Support

Dorothy Sutton

Mesh Flood is a free iOS app for a screen-print shop log. Flood an empty mesh from the top edge to the bottom edge, then print from the bottom edge to the top edge to save a sheet. A second flood clogs the mesh, and only a wash returns it to empty. A stroke that lifts inside the mesh does not count. Questions about the app: dorothy.sutton44@sina.com.
