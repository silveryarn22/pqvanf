Mesh Flood Privacy Policy

Dorothy Sutton

Mesh Flood keeps each screen name, whether the mesh is empty, coated, or clogged, and the sheets printed from it on this iPhone. An empty mesh becomes one coat only after a squeegee stroke enters the top edge and leaves the bottom edge without lifting. A coated mesh saves a sheet only after a stroke enters the bottom edge and leaves the top edge without lifting. A second flood clogs the mesh. A stroke that lifts inside the mesh is not recorded. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing a sheet uses the system share sheet and only sends the text you choose to share.
