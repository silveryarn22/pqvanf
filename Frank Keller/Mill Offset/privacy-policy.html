Mill Offset Privacy Policy

Frank Keller

Mill Offset keeps each job name, the cutter radius, and the closed path vertices on this iPhone. It draws the inward offset and writes whether the path will clear or will gouge. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing a path uses the system share sheet and only sends the text you choose to share.
