Mill Offset Support

Frank Keller

Mill Offset is a free iOS app for checking a closed milling path against a cutter radius. Draw or type the vertices in millimetres, set the radius, and the app offsets the path inward. If offset edges that do not share a corner cross, or an offset edge turns back on itself, the path is marked Will gouge and kept. Otherwise it is marked Will clear.
