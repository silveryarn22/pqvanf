Helen Zimmerman

Hoop Drop keeps one barrel circle and one hoop on this iPhone. Closing the circle does not heat the hoop. Heating the hoop does not close the circle and does not seat it. Dropping seats the hoop only when the circle is already closed and the hoop is still hot. Dropping while the circle is open or the hoop is cold changes nothing. After the hoop stays on, trying to open the circle leaves it closed and leaves the hoop on. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing the hoop uses the system share sheet and only sends the text you choose to share.
