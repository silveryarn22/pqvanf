Support for Scythe Peen

Scythe Peen is a free iOS app by Frank Crawford. Email frank.crawford13@163.com.

A blade has four stations. Draw the first line before the second line will stay. A strike on the back, on a line that is already there, or on the second line before the first one, springs back and leaves the station as it was.

Blades stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not swing the hammer for you. Questions: frank.crawford13@163.com.
