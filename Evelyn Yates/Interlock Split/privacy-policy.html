Interlock Split keeps each safety chain's name and its continuity readings (which two terminals were probed, beep or silent, and which contact was repaired) on this iPhone. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing the readings uses the system share sheet and only sends the text you choose to share.
