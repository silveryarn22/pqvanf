Evelyn Yates

Interlock Split is a free iOS app for finding an open contact in a series safety chain with a continuity tester, with the power isolated. Each reading between two terminals clears or keeps a span of contacts, and the app names the next pair to probe until one contact is left. After a repair, test the whole chain end to end.
