Batik Dip Privacy Policy

Ruth Sutton

Batik Dip keeps each cloth on this iPhone. Wax holds the color a patch already has. Only a darker bath dyes the open patches. Boiling removes the wax: three or more shades come out patterned, and fewer come out flat. After the boil, the cloth does not change again. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing uses the system share sheet and only sends the cloth name, the bath count, and whether it is patterned, flat, or not boiled.
