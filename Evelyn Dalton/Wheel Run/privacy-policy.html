Privacy Policy

Wheel Run is an offline tool for scoring a sheet of glass from one edge to the other. It does not track you.

The sheets you name stay on this iPhone. The app does not require an account. There is nothing to buy. The app does not collect personal data and does not send your sheets anywhere.
