Support for Pivot Broach

Pivot Broach is a free iOS app by Albert Keller. Email albert.keller73@126.com.

A quarter turn seats the hole or opens the shake only after that hole's shake has been read. An unread turn springs back. A hole that is seated with one shake locks. Another turn would oval the hole and springs back.

Plates stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not broach the plate for you. Questions: albert.keller73@126.com.
