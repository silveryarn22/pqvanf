Hump Throw Privacy Policy

Alice Underwood

Hump Throw keeps one lump of clay on this iPhone. The lump centers only when the wheel is spinning and the hump is pressed across the center. A still wheel leaves the hump where it is. Opening before the lump is centered throws the clay off. Opening after it is centered keeps a floor. Pulling up adds one wall step, up to three. Another pull at the third step cracks the rim and leaves the height where it is. The rim can be rounded only when it is not cracked. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing uses the system share sheet and only sends the text you choose to share.
