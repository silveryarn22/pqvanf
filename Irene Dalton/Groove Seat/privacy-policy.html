Groove Seat Privacy Policy

Irene Dalton

Groove Seat keeps each board on this iPhone. A groove and a string are each 1, 2, or 3. Equal widths sit the string and clear the gap. A narrower string leaves a gap. A wider string stays on the bench. The plane flushes a seated string, and tears out a gapped string. After the surface is flush, the widths stay. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing uses the system share sheet and only sends the board name and whether the surface is flush.
