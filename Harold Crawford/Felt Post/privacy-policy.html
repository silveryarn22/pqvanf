Felt Post Privacy Policy

Harold Crawford

Felt Post keeps one mould and its felts on this iPhone. An empty mould takes pulp when you dip it. Shaking while water is still in forms the sheet. Couching onto an empty felt lays that sheet down and empties the mould. Couching before the sheet is formed leaves the sheet on the mould. Couching onto a felt that already holds a sheet changes nothing. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing a couched sheet uses the system share sheet and only sends the text you choose to share.
