Support for Cap Iron

Cap Iron is a free iOS app by Irene Nolan. Email irene.nolan59@sina.com.

A plane has four faces. The cap iron has to sit one step behind the edge before the edge may come out one step and leave a shaving. Covering the edge, sitting two steps back, or running the edge out two steps springs back and leaves the face as it was.

Planes stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not push the plane for you. Questions: irene.nolan59@sina.com.
