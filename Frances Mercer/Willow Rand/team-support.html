Willow Rand Support

Frances Mercer

Willow Rand keeps one round of six stakes on this iPhone. The first stroke covers the first stake only from the front. The next stake is covered only when the face changes. The same face slips, and that stake stays bare. Six covered stakes close the round. Press the border only after the round is closed. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing uses the system share sheet and only sends the text you choose to share.
